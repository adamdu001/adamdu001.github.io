Anbang Du is a data scientist with experience designing reproducible ML/LLM workflows and large-scale scientific data pipelines. He is an advanced Python practitioner working across biomedical embeddings, model evaluation, human-in-the-loop classification and retrieval-augmented generation prototypes. He has built fault-tolerant systems processing tens of millions of grants, publications, clinical trials and patents, with experience leading multidisciplinary teams and translating technical work for research and policy stakeholders.

Anbang is currently a final-stage PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Southampton (Agent, Interaction, and Complexity Group, School of Electronics and Computer Science). He is also a visiting researcher at Harvard University, Health System Innovation Lab. He is interested in computational social science, network science, and science of science. His research involves (i) mapping health research funding landscape, (ii) modelling fragility and resilience under shock, and (iii) research evaluation including interdisciplinarity, spillovers, and policy uptakes. 

Anbang has published first-author research articles in The Lancet Oncology, Journal of Informetrics, and Applied Network Science. He has also given talks at the 15th Annual Conference on Complex Networks (CompleNet 2024) and at France’s International Conference on Complex Systems (FRCCS 2025). 

Anbang’s work is shaping policy conversations globally and has been featured by dozens of international media outlets. He has delivered invited briefings to the World Health Organization (2024, 2025), Wellcome Trust (2025), NIHR (2025), and the Commonwealth Secretariat (2025) on health research investment, network, and system resilience under geopolitical shocks.

Anbang is the co-founder of [AInotate](https://www.ainotate.co), a research intelligence solution matching funding opportunities and automating application workflows for researchers and providing agile portfolio intelligence for institutions. AInotate was selected for presentation at Dubai's Prototypes for Humanity 2025 and invited for competition at the Havard HSIL Hackathon 2026. Anbang is also building startup projects in EdTech and DatingTech. Feel free to book a quick chat (button on the left).
